CORE CAPABILITIES

The food and the system around it.

A restaurant cannot separate the menu from labor, equipment, purchasing, training, timing, and repeatability. The consulting work treats them as one operating system.

01

Menu & Concept Development

Menu architecture, dish development, seasonal refinement, concept alignment, presentation, practical execution, and menu simplification where complexity is doing more harm than good.

02

Menu Engineering & Food Cost

Recipe costing, portion logic, ingredient overlap, yield awareness, pricing inputs, menu mix review, and identifying items that create operational or margin drag.

OPERATIONAL REVIEW

Find the constraint before prescribing the fix.

An operational review can start with the menu, kitchen flow, prep, purchasing, labor deployment, training, consistency, or guest-facing execution. The point is not to generate a giant binder. It is to identify what is actually creating friction and establish a prioritized path forward.

That may lead to a focused menu project, a systems reset, sourcing work, training documentation, or a broader opening or relaunch engagement. Scope follows the problem instead of the other way around.
